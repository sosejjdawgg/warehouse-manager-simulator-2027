Privacy Policy

Warehouse Manager (Alpha) · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Warehouse Manager ("the Game") handles information. The Game is made by James Scott ("we", "us"), who is responsible for it under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: jamscoot22@gmail.com

The short version

We run no servers and do not collect, receive or sell personal information. The Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no online features. It makes no network requests of its own.

What is stored, and where

To work, the Game saves a few things on your own device only, in your browser's local storage:

  • your career save (company, staff, the names and looks you create, results and settings in the game);
  • your sound setting.

This information stays on your device and we cannot see it. Please don't type your real full name, or anyone else's, into the Game.

Playing in a web browser

If you play on a website, the host serving the Game (for example itch.io) may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as all websites do. That is covered by the host's own privacy policy. The Game itself sends nothing to us.

Children

Because the Game depicts serious workplace accidents, including deaths, it is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Deleting your data

Use Abandon career in the Game's settings, or clear the site data for the Game in your browser (or uninstall the app). This removes everything the Game has saved.

Your rights

You have rights under UK data protection law, including to ask what information we hold about you. As we hold none, there is nothing for us to give or delete, but you can contact us at jamscoot22@gmail.com with any question. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If a future version adds online features, ads or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the Game will ask you to review it.
